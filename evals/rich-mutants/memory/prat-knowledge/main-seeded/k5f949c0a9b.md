A renderable yields `Segment`s or nested renderables, and `Console.render` recurses into anything that is not a Segment. Classes such as `Padding`, `Constrain`, `Columns`, `Live`, `Markdown` and `Emoji` inherit `JupyterMixin` for notebook display. Without `__rich_measure__`, `Measurement.get` assumes `(0, max_width)`, so containers like `Padding` and `Constrain` must delegate measurement to their child.
