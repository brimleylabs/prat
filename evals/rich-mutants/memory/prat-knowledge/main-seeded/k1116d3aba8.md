`Console.is_terminal` checks `force_terminal`, then idlelib, Jupyter, `TTY_COMPATIBLE`, `FORCE_COLOR`, and then `isatty()`. `_detect_color_system` reads `COLORTERM` and `TERM`, and `NO_COLOR`, `COLUMNS`, `LINES`, `TTY_INTERACTIVE` and `JUPYTER_COLUMNS` are read in `__init__` or `size`. `Console(_environ=...)` replaces `os.environ` for tests. `diagnose.report` lists the variables that matter.
