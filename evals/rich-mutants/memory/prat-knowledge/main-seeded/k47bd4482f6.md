`TerminalTheme(background, foreground, normal, bright)` builds a 16-colour `Palette` from `normal + (bright or normal)`. It defines DEFAULT_TERMINAL_THEME, MONOKAI, DIMMED_MONOKAI, NIGHT_OWLISH and SVG_EXPORT_THEME. MONOKAI, DIMMED_MONOKAI, NIGHT_OWLISH and SVG_EXPORT_THEME pass 9 entries in `normal` and 7 in `bright`. That sums to 16, but it is not the 8+8 split the docstring describes.
