This part of the `rich` package holds the rendering primitives and several high-level widgets. The primitives are `Segment` (`segment.py`), `Style` (`style.py`), `Region`, `Styled`, `Screen`, `protocol.py` (`rich_cast` and `is_renderable`) and `repr.py` (the `rich_repr` protocol). The widgets are `Panel`, `Rule`, `Syntax` (a Pygments wrapper), `Pretty` with `pretty_repr` and `pprint`, `Progress` with `ProgressBar`, `Spinner` and `Status`, `Prompt`, and `render_scope`. `Style` and `Segment` sit at the bottom of the render pipeline, so changes to them affect almost everything else. The widgets mostly implement `__rich_console__` and `__rich_measure__`, usually through `JupyterMixin`, and compose each other: `Status` uses `Live` and `Spinner`, `Progress` uses `Live`, `Table`, `ProgressBar` and `Spinner`, and `scope.py` uses `Panel`, `Table` and `Pretty`. Most files end with an `if __name__ == "__main__"` demo block, marked `# pragma: no cover` in most of them, and `py.typed` is an empty marker file.
