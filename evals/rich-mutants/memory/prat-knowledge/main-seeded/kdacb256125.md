`make_progress` and `test_columns` inject `get_time` (an incrementing `fake_time` or `MockClock`) and set `auto_refresh=False`. Elapsed and remaining times such as `0:00:07` are baked into expected strings. Adding or removing a `get_time()` call, or an extra refresh, changes those values. `test_progress_max_refresh` checks that `column.max_refresh` throttles redraws.
