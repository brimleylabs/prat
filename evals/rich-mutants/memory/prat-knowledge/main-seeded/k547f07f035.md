Style uses `__slots__`, and its fields (`_color`, `_bgcolor`, `_attributes`, `_set_attributes`, `_link`, `_link_id`, `_meta` and others) are copied by hand in `from_color`, `from_meta`, `without_color`, `copy`, `clear_meta_and_links`, `update_link` and `_add`. A new attribute or slot must be added to all of them and to `_hash_getter`. `__eq__` and `__hash__` compare only the hash of that tuple. `parse`, `normalize`, `_add`, `get_html_style` and `clear_meta_and_links` use `lru_cache`. Styles with a link or meta get a fresh `link_id` from a global counter, and `__add__` copies the result when a link is present.
