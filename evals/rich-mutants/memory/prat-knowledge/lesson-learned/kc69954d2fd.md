They use calls such as Console.print_exception(max_frames=..., show_locals=...), Live(screen=..., redirect_stderr=...), `__rich__` renderables (Header and Clock), `Table.grid`, `console.measure`, `Console.export_*` and `save_*`, `@rich.repr.auto`, `@group()`, and `OverflowMethod`. export.py relies on `export_html(clear=False)` to keep the console buffer. A rename or signature change in the `rich/` package can break an example silently, because nothing tests them.
