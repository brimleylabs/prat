`.coveragerc` omits rich/jupyter.py, rich/_windows.py, rich/_timer.py and rich/diagnose.py from the run, and excludes `pragma: no cover`, `if TYPE_CHECKING:`, `if __name__ == "__main__":` and `@overload` lines from the report. Don't rely on the coverage report to catch regressions in the omitted files. CI uploads coverage but does not fail on it, so a coverage number is not a gate.
