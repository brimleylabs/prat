Imports are from `rich` and its documented submodules. Some use `from rich import print` instead of the builtin. Customisation goes through public hooks: `__rich__` (Header and Clock classes in fullscreen.py and layout.py), `RegexHighlighter` with `base_style` and `highlights` plus a `Theme` (highlighter.py, log.py), `Highlighter.highlight` (rainbow.py), and the `@rich.repr.auto` decorator (repr.py). A change to those APIs can break these scripts.
