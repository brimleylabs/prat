`.coveragerc` omits rich/jupyter.py, rich/_windows.py, rich/_timer.py and rich/diagnose.py from measurement. It also excludes lines with `pragma: no cover`, `if TYPE_CHECKING:`, `if __name__ == "__main__":` and `@overload`, which is why __main__ demos carry the pragma. Low coverage numbers for those files are expected, so do not rely on the coverage report to find untested demo code.
