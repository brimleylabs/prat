Panel, Rule, Screen, Styled, Spinner, ProgressBar, Syntax and Pretty follow this protocol, with `Console`, `ConsoleOptions` and `RenderResult` imported only under `TYPE_CHECKING` where circular imports are a risk. `__rich_measure__` must agree with the render path: Panel measures its child plus padding plus 2 border cells, and Rule returns `Measurement(1, 1)`. Changing one without the other breaks `expand=False` layouts.
