The tools/ directory holds standalone developer scripts for Rich. These are not part of the shipped package, and tools/README.md says they "aren't for general use". They do three jobs. `make_emoji.py` and `make_width_tables.py` regenerate data files. `profile_divide.py`, `profile_pretty.py` and `stress_test_pretty.py` are ad-hoc benchmarks of `Segment.divide`, `Pretty` and `Panel`. `movies.md` is a sample markdown document with tables. The scripts import from rich (`cells`, `segment`, `console`, `pretty`, `panel`, `_timer`). `make_width_tables.py` writes into `rich/_unicode_data/`. `tools/cats.json` is the input for `profile_pretty.py`.
