`Traceback.__init__` takes `locals_overlow` (stored as `self.locals_overflow`), while `install` and `from_exception` expose `locals_overflow` and translate it when constructing. Keep the spelling consistent if you touch these signatures, or direct callers of `Traceback(...)` will break. `extract` is also called recursively for exception groups. That recursive call passes only `locals_max_length`, `locals_hide_dunder` and `locals_hide_sunder`, not `locals_max_string` or `locals_max_depth`.
