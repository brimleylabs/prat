`cells.py` short-circuits through `_is_single_cell_widths` (a frozenset of known 1-cell characters). `cell_len` uses an `lru_cache` only for strings shorter than 512 characters. Otherwise it walks characters, treating ZWJ (`\u200d`) and VS16 (`\ufe0f`) specially via `narrow_to_wide` from `rich._unicode_data`. `split_graphemes`, `_split_text`, `set_cell_size` and `chop_cells` must agree with `_cell_len`. Splitting inside a double-width character replaces the halves with spaces. Width tables are loaded through `load_cell_table(unicode_version)`, where `unicode_version` can be `"auto"` or `"latest"`.
