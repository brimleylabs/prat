`make_width_tables.py` calls `CellTable(version, table, frozenset(...))` and reads `.unicode_version`, `.widths` and `.narrow_to_wide`. The emitted files also call `CellTable(...)`. Changing that class's fields or signature in rich/cells.py breaks the generator and every generated file.
