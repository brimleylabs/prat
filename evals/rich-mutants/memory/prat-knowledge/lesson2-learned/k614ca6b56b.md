The title is the dotted module name, such as `rich.panel`, and the `=` underline matches its length. The body is `.. automodule:: rich.<name>` followed by an indented `:members:` option; bare `:members:` documents everything (table.rst, tree.rst), while `:members: Panel` or `:members: RichHandler` limits it to named classes (panel.rst, logging.rst). A page may also start with a `.. _label:` line, as logging.rst does.
