rich/__init__.py exposes `get_console` (lazily creates the global Console), `reconfigure`, `print`, `print_json` and `inspect`, and re-exports `load_ipython_extension` from `_extension.py`. Other files here are small renderables (`align.py`, `bar.py`, `box.py`), ANSI decoding (`ansi.py`), the Windows legacy-console layer (`_win32_console.py`, `_windows.py`, `_windows_renderer.py`), and internal helpers for emoji, spinners, palettes, ratio/wrap math, loops and stacks, `_inspect.py` and `_log_render.py`. Many files end with an `if __name__ == "__main__"` demo block.
