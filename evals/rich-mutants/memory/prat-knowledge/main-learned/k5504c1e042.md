CONTRIBUTING.md requires a CHANGELOG.md entry for every user-facing change. CHANGELOG.md follows Keep a Changelog: add new entries under `## Unreleased` in `### Added`, `### Changed` or `### Fixed`, usually with a PR link. First-time contributors add their name to CONTRIBUTORS.md, sorted alphabetically by surname.
