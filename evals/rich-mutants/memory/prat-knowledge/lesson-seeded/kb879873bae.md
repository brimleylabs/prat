Every page has the title `rich.<module>` (`rich` for init.rst), an `=` underline, a blank line, then `.. automodule:: rich.<module>` with an indented `:members:` option. Copy this shape for new pages. The underline is at least as long as the title, and the files are named after the module (e.g. `progress_bar.rst` for `rich.progress_bar`).
