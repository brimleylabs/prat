Use cell_len, set_cell_size, chop_cells and split_text. Cell widths come from version-specific Unicode tables in rich/_unicode_data, chosen by UNICODE_VERSION ('auto' reads the env var) and clamped to the nearest lower version. Wrapping, padding, tables and segments all depend on this.
