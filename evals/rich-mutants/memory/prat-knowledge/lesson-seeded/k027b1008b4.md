`Text`, `Table` and `Tree` subclass `JupyterMixin` and yield `Segment`s from `__rich_console__`. They report widths through `__rich_measure__`, which returns a `Measurement(min, max)`. Imports from `rich.console` are guarded by `TYPE_CHECKING` in `text.py` and `table.py` to avoid circular imports. `tree.py` and `traceback.py` import `rich.console` directly.
