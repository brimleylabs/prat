The table runs from 0 to 255 but omits numbers that duplicate another name. For example it goes from 18 to 20 and from 22 to 25. The first 16 rows have no Hex or RGB because the terminal defines them, and the page says they may render differently. This is deliberate, so do not add rows to fill the gaps.
