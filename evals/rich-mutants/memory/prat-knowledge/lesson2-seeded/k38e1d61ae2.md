This is part 3 of the `rich` package. It holds the rendering building blocks that sit between `Console` and the terminal: `Segment`, `Style`, `Panel`, `Rule`, `Syntax`, `Pretty`, `Screen`, `Styled`, `Scope` and the `Palette` colour matcher. It also holds the interactive and animated pieces: `Progress` with its columns and `ProgressBar`, `Spinner`, `Status`, and `Prompt`. Helpers include `protocol.py` (`rich_cast`/`is_renderable`), `repr.py` (the `@rich_repr` decorator) and `region.py`. Most renderables implement `__rich_console__` and often `__rich_measure__`, and most subclass `JupyterMixin`. They depend on `console.py`, `text.py`, `measure.py`, `cells.py`, `color.py` and `table.py`, and many are imported back into `console.py` lazily. Nearly every file ends in an `if __name__ == "__main__":  # pragma: no cover` demo.
