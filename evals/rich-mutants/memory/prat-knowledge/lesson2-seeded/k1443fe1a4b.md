Rich cannot know how wide a character renders on a given terminal, so Table and Panel alignment can break with emoji. Multi-codepoint characters such as flags and modifiers are worst. The advice is to stick to emoji from Unicode version 9 or earlier. The spinner answer repeats this for `python -m rich.spinner`, especially on Windows.
