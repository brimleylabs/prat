`tests/render.py` has `render(renderable, no_wrap=False)`, which prints at width 100 with truecolor and `legacy_windows=False`. Its `replace_link_ids` regex rewrites hyperlink `id=...;path` to `id=0;foo`, because the real ids are random and path-dependent. test_card.py, test_log.py, test_markdown.py and test_markdown_no_hyperlinks.py each re-declare `re_link_ids` and `replace_link_ids` instead of importing them. In test_log.py the `test_log.py` filename in the output is also replaced with `source.py`.
