`extract` skips frames whose locals contain `_rich_traceback_omit`. A frame with `_rich_traceback_guard` clears the frames collected so far. It follows `__cause__`, then `__context__` unless `__suppress_context__` is set, and handles ExceptionGroup on 3.11+. `max_frames` is clamped to at least 4 (0 means no limit) and hides the middle frames. `suppress` paths are normalized with `abspath`. Relative filenames are joined with `rich._IMPORT_CWD`. Error-range underlining uses `co_positions()` on 3.11+ and is guarded against out-of-range lines.
