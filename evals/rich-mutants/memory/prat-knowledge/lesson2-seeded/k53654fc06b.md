`Tree.__rich_console__` and `__rich_measure__` use iterator stacks instead of recursion, so deep trees do not hit recursion limits. Guide glyphs come from `TREE_GUIDES`. A bold guide style selects the heavy set, `underline2` selects the double set, `legacy_windows` forces the plain set, and `ascii_only` uses `ASCII_GUIDES`. `Tree.add` inherits `style`, `guide_style` and `highlight` from its parent unless they are overridden. Measurement assumes 4 cells of indent per level. `TerminalTheme` takes 8 normal and 8 bright colors and builds a `Palette`. `SVG_EXPORT_THEME` and `DEFAULT_TERMINAL_THEME` are used for export.
