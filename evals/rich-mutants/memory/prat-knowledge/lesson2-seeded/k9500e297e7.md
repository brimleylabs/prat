`ratio_resolve` in `rich/_ratio.py` can return sizes summing to more than `total` when minimums cannot be met, and callers such as `Layout` rely on clipping. `ratio_distribute` asserts that the ratios sum to more than 0. `divide_line` in `rich/_wrap.py` measures in cell width, not `len`, and `fold=False` crops rather than wraps long words. `AnsiDecoder` in `rich/ansi.py` is deliberately lenient: it ignores invalid SGR codes, clamps them to 255 and keeps style state across lines.
