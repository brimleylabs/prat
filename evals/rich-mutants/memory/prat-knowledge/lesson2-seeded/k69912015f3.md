`rich/_win32_console.py` raises `ImportError` on non-Windows platforms and wraps kernel32 calls with ctypes `argtypes`/`restype`. `rich/_windows.py` catches that error and falls back to a `WindowsConsoleFeatures` with `vt=False` and `truecolor=False`. Truecolor requires VT processing plus Windows 10 build >= 15063. `rich/_windows_renderer.py` translates `Segment` control codes into `LegacyWindowsTerm` calls for consoles without VT. Windows coordinates are (row, col), enforced through the `WindowsCoordinates` NamedTuple, and `ControlType` positions from `Segment` are 1-based, hence the `-1` conversions.
